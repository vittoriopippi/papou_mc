Papou HUD for **Minecraft 26.3**. Players with 1.4.0 or later get this update from the popup at startup.

## New: mob health in hearts

Mobs within 20 blocks show their health over their heads, in hearts like your own health bar (half hearts too). Mobs with more than 10 hearts, like Endermen and Iron Golems, show a heart and the numbers instead ("31.5/50"). The hearts sit above the mob's name if it has one, and don't show through walls. Turn it on or off in the settings menu (`M`), row **Mob health**.

## New: furnace XP on Paper servers

The stored XP in furnaces, smokers and blast furnaces (and the level you'd reach by taking it) only exists on the server, so it needs Papou HUD there. Until now only the mod (singleplayer, Essential, Fabric servers) sent it; now the **Paper plugin** sends it too. Update the plugin on the server to `papouhud-paper-1.4.6.jar`; nothing changes for players.

## Files

- `papouhud-1.4.6.jar`: the mod, for players (and for Fabric servers).
- `papouhud-paper-1.4.6.jar`: the plugin for Paper 26.3 servers. It doesn't go in `.minecraft/mods`.
