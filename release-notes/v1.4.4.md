Papou HUD for **Minecraft 26.3**. Players with 1.4.0 or later get this update from the popup at startup.

## New: one skull per death

Each death now gets its own skull on the locator bar, in its own color (red, yellow, aqua, purple, green, orange), so if you die again on the way back to your items you can find both spots. Up to 6 are kept.

- **As long as your items last:** items on the ground vanish after 5 minutes, but their clock only runs while a player is within the server's simulation distance (about 100 blocks on a server set to 6 chunks). The skulls now follow the same rule: far from everyone, the items and the skull both wait.
- **Gone when you pick them up:** when you get close and no items are left around the spot, the skull goes away.
- **Saved:** the skulls stay when you leave the game, and a chat line lists them when you come back.

## New: furnace XP shows the level you'd reach

Under the stored XP, furnaces, smokers and blast furnaces now show the level you would reach by taking it (e.g. "15.4 XP", then "→ lv 23" on the line below). The tooltip shows your level now and after, and warns you when damaged gear with Mending would take the XP first.

## Files

- `papouhud-1.4.4.jar`: the mod, for players (and for Fabric servers).
- `papouhud-paper-1.4.4.jar`: the plugin for Paper 26.3 servers. It doesn't go in `.minecraft/mods`.
